Why I'm building it

When you run payroll in a new place, a search bar isn't much help. Search assumes you already know what to ask.

I wanted a checklist instead. Start with the global questions, like whether the employee is classified correctly. Then drill into the local ones, like which social contribution bands apply. Every rule links back to the law or guidance it came from.

It's built for the HR or payroll person doing the work, not for employees looking up their own pay.

Notes to past me

Things I wish someone had told me before I started.

Newer isn't always better

The newest county boundaries are the full-detail version. On a map this size they look the same as the generalized file, but the load times aren't even close. I went with the generalized one, even though it's a little older.

Read the prompt before you type

A few commands ran in the wrong folder before I made checking the terminal prompt a habit.

Commit small, commit often

Small commits with plain messages make it easy to see what changed and to back out of a bad idea.
